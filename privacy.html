To Scale Uploader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

To Scale Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the "To Scale" TikTok account (@toscalefacts) to send the owner's own videos to their TikTok drafts.

1. Data we access

When the owner authorizes the app with TikTok, the app receives basic account information (user.info.basic), used only to confirm which account is connected; permission to upload videos as drafts (video.upload); and an access token issued by TikTok.

2. How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok drafts. Nothing is published automatically: the owner reviews and publishes each video inside the TikTok app.

3. Storage

The access token is stored only on the owner's personal computer. It is not stored on any external server and is not shared with anyone.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties. No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used.

5. Other users

The app does not collect data from any other TikTok users or from viewers of the videos.

6. Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions). Deleting the local token removes all stored data.
